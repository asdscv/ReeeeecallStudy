Refund Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026 (policy version 2026-07-29)

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work for purchases made on ReeeeecallStudy ("the Service"). ReeeeecallStudy sells digital products only. Web payments are processed by our authorized reseller and Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy; in-app purchases on mobile are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Prices are charged in US dollars.

1. The refund window

Purchases are refundable for 14 days from the date of the charge, subject to the conditions in sections 2 and 3 below. This single window is used worldwide and is at least as long as the statutory cooling-off period in every market we sell to, including Korea (7 days) and the EU/UK (14 days).

2. AI credit packs

AI credit packs are one-time purchases of a consumable balance used for AI-powered card generation. Credits are delivered to your account the moment the purchase settles.

  • Fully unused pack: refundable in full within 14 days of purchase, provided no credits have been spent from your balance since that purchase.
  • Any credits spent: not refundable. Once credits are spent the corresponding AI generation has already been delivered and cannot be returned.
  • No partial refunds. A pack is refunded in full or not at all.

Before you buy, we ask you to confirm that the credits are made available immediately and that spending any of them ends your right to withdraw from the purchase. We record that confirmation with your order.

3. Subscriptions

Paid subscriptions (increased card-storage plans) are billed monthly and renew automatically until cancelled. You may cancel at any time; access continues until the end of the current paid period, after which no further charges are made. Cancelling does not by itself trigger a refund.

  • First charge: refundable in full within 14 days, provided the paid benefit is unused — that is, your account still holds no more cards than the free plan allows, so nothing you built depends on the paid plan.
  • Renewal charges: not refundable. Cancel before a renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • No proration. We do not refund the unused remainder of a period; you keep access until the period ends.

4. Where to request a refund

Who issues the refund depends on where you bought. This is not our choice — the app stores reserve refunds for themselves.

iPhone / iPad (App Store) Apple issues all App Store refunds and decides each request. Use the “Request a refund” link in the app, or visit reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue an App Store refund on your behalf, but write to us and we will support your request.

We aim to respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method by whichever processor handled the purchase.

If a refund is approved, access ends. Refunding a credit pack removes those credits from your balance, and refunding a subscription ends the paid plan immediately, including the raised card limit. Cards above the free limit are not deleted, but you will not be able to add more until you are back within it.

5. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any refund or cancellation right you have under the consumer-protection law of your country, and where such a right applies it takes precedence over the conditions above. In Korea and the EU/UK the right to withdraw from a purchase of digital content may be restricted once you begin using that content, which is why we ask you to confirm the notice described in sections 2 and 3 before you buy.
