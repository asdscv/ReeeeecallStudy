## 긴 설명 (양쪽 공통)
ReeeeecallStudy is an intelligent flashcard application that helps you memorize anything effectively using spaced repetition science (SRS).

**6 Study Modes**
• SRS Review — Scientifically optimized review scheduling
• Sequential Review — Learn new cards, then review in batches
• Random — Shuffle and study
• Sequential — Go through cards in order, resuming where you left off
• By Date — Study cards added on a specific date
• Cramming — Rapid-fire rounds until every card is mastered

**Powerful Features**
• Create decks with customizable card templates
• Import and export cards via CSV or JSON
• Track your progress with detailed study statistics
• Daily study streaks and mastery tracking
• Heatmap visualization of your study activity
• AI card generation — enter a topic and get a template, deck and cards
• AI quizzes — multiple choice, short answer and essay questions made from your own cards, graded by AI
• Study goals and a daily plan built from your decks
• Text-to-Speech in 8 languages for pronunciation practice

**Marketplace**
• Browse and discover shared decks from the community
• Publish your own decks to help others learn

**Cross-Platform**
• Web app + iOS + Android
• Your data syncs seamlessly across all devices
• Interface in 8 languages: English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Spanish

**Pricing**
• Free to start — every account gets 10 AI cards and 5 AI quiz questions every day, and can store up to 5,000 cards
• Standard subscription ($3.99/month) raises the card limit to 100,000
• AI credit packs ($0.99 / $4.99 / $9.99) for AI use beyond the daily free allowance
• AI runs on our servers — no API key needed and none is asked for

**Your data**
• Export your cards, decks and study history to CSV or JSON anytime
• Account deletion available in settings

Start learning smarter today with ReeeeecallStudy!
